# D-0002 — The Explorer, a personal submersible

Rusty, on the booth photos of the first shape (2026-10-07): "I was hoping for the submersible to be
more of a slick personal submersible, less of a military submarine. Looks good otherwise though."
The sail and the long cigar hull of D-0001, Alvin's, read as a submarine's.

**Decision**:

- **The hull** is low and white (`#e9e8e1`, dyeable): 4.9 long, 1.84 across, 1.1 deep, a rounded
  bow and a tail that tapers between the pontoons; its section a superellipse (exponent 2.4).
- **The bubble** covers the cabin: an ellipsoid 1.76 across and 3.2 long, its crown 2.2 up, cut
  away at the hull's waist and kept inside its beam, so the dark rim where glass meets hull runs
  clean all round. Glass wherever it stands clear of the hull, named in the profile's `cockpit` too
  (Vanilla Wheels' D-0031): from inside it is not drawn.
- **Four aboard, two by two**, the pilot in front on the left; every eye 1.67 up, clear of the
  hull's shoulder (1.40), so everyone looks out all round, not through a port.
- **Two pontoons** along its sides, joined to the hull by a web: white with a teal stripe along each
  outer flank (the one colour a dye leaves), the **floods in their noses** (range 24), a **ducted
  screw** at each tail carried by four fins, the two turning opposite ways; skids under them.
- **The hatch** in the bubble's crown is where a rider gets out; **the hold's hatch** is on the
  deck behind the bubble, the hold's three rows hidden in the solid tail under it, as before.
- **Two torpedo tubes** under the chin, the profile's two weapons.
- **Hit boxes**: the body's own box stands to the deck's height only, the bubble has two of its own
  (the after one stopping short of the hold's hatch), and the tail's covers the hatch and the ducts.
  A click aimed down at the hatch must land at the deck's height: landing on a box as high as the
  bubble, it left the chest 1.6 blocks along the line, past the 1.5 Vanilla Wheels follows a click
  for one, and nothing opened (the gametest's first run on this shape).
- **The draft is 0.40**: surfaced, the waterline is just over the pontoons, half a metre of white
  hull and the whole bubble out. At 0.52, the booth's photo showed a bubble afloat, the hull all
  under the surface.
- 540 boxes (D-0001's was 712); the screws, the hull, the bubble and the pontoons on the lattice of
  2, 1 and 2 voxels as before.

**Why**: a bubble over the crew is what a personal submersible is (Triton's, U-Boat Worx's), and
the Scout's bubble makes the two a family; a sail is a submarine's.

**Alternatives rejected**:
- *Graphite pontoons*: rendered beside the white hull they read as a tank's tracks.
- *Vertical fins over the ducts*: from ahead they stood up like antennae.
- *The bubble as wide as the hull*: it bulged past the side amidships and the rim dipped there.

**Verification**:
- GameTests (8) on the new shape: the profiles; the recipes; a crafted Explorer set down on open
  water floats at its draft; it holds its depth, then floats; its top speed; four ride and breathe
  at depth, a fifth refused; the hold through the deck hatch and from a seat; a torpedo from each
  tube under the chin.
- The three data mutations, each caught. The hatch test aims at the hatch's after end, where only
  the tail's box covers it: aimed at its front, the body's own box covered it too and the tail's
  box's removal would have gone unnoticed.
- The booth (17 checks): it floats at its draft with its white hull showing, a real Left Shift dives
  it, the screws turn, the pilot sees the water ahead through the bubble (not drawn for the rider),
  a back seat's eye sees out to the side, the floods light the seabed at night, a torpedo, R at the
  crown's hatch, the fit-out's four and two slots, the hold's three rows.
