# D-0001 — The Explorer

nfx's brief (2026-10-07, Submersibles' `knowledge/sources/`): a four-seat submarine, slow and tough,
a 9×3 hold, wide twin floods, four upgrade and two weapon slots; "a fat three-window research sub,
twin floods on a chin pod, a visible hold hatch, two stern props"; crafted at a crafting table.
Rusty made it a Vanilla Wheels vehicle on a protocol of its own (Submersibles' D-0001); the
helicopters are life-size, so this is too.

**Decision**:

- **Shape.** One block a metre, in the proportions of Alvin (DSV-2): a blunt rounded bow, a body
  2.6 across and 2.4 deep, a tapering stern, a dark sail forward of amidships rising a metre over
  the deck, skids; white from the factory (`#e9e8e1`), dyeable, the sail dark. Read off a
  public-domain three-view and photograph (`devtools/art/reference/SOURCES.md`). What is nfx's: the
  three ports, the twin floods on a chin pod, the hold's hatch, two screws (Alvin has one).
- **A voxel shell** (the shared `tools/bbgen/voxel.py`, made for it): the hull is a superellipse
  section (exponent 2.6) swept along the length, hollowed in three dimensions so the bow is a
  shell like the rest (an inset taken slice by slice left the blunt bow open), on a lattice of 2, 1
  and 2 voxels across, up and along. One voxel everywhere was smoother but 1322 boxes (5800 faces a
  frame, every one emitted by the CPU each frame by Vanilla Wheels' drawer); two everywhere was 477
  but terraced the top in plain contour lines; the 1-voxel step up and down keeps those fine for
  712 boxes, between the Huey's 446 and the Chinook's 635. A greedy face mesher was measured and
  made more quads than boxes, so boxes it is, with every hidden face left out.
- **The ports**: the bow's 0.92 across (the pilot's), one 0.68 across in each side (the observers'),
  a window in the sail's front and two small ports in its sides (the fourth rider's), each glazed
  and ringed a voxel proud. Every seat's `eye` is at its glass. The glass is named in the profile's
  `cockpit` as well (Vanilla Wheels' D-0031, made for this): it is not drawn from a rider's own
  eyes, so the view out is clear, where its stepped faces stacked across the view before.
- **Seats.** The pilot, then an observer at each side port, then the fourth on a stool in the sail.
  Riders sit on the cabin floor, as Alvin's crew do.
- **The hold**: a double chest's three rows at half size, hidden in the solid stern under a hatch
  on the after deck, a voxel proud of the hull; the stern's hit box covers the hatch, so a click on
  it reaches the chest, and the inventory key opens it from a seat (as the Huey's store, its D-0002).
- **Fittings**: the chin pod with the floods (range 24) on its front and a torpedo tube along each
  side, the muzzles the profile's two weapons; two screws turning opposite ways in shrouds on the
  tips of swept stern planes, a small swept rudder above and below; an antenna on the sail. Fittings
  are cut at the hull's skin (the shell's envelope), so none stands in the cabin.
- **The draft is 0.45**: at the surface the waterline crosses the ports, a metre of white hull and
  the sail out. At first it was 0.72, Alvin's, and the booth's photo showed a submarine all but
  under water, the hull blue through the surface.
- **Numbers**: nfx's proposed stats in Submersibles' terms: top speed 0.22 blocks a tick (thrust
  0.011, slip 0.016, hull drag 0.032, the slip a third of the drag so the Enhanced Propeller gives
  a third, not four times); climb 0.08; turn 1.8; spool three seconds; fuel 1.2 a tick of 36 000;
  durability 4; crashes safe to 0.18, a wreck at 1.0; currents 0.4. Tuned in the playtest.
- **Crafting** (nfx: no workshop): the hull at a crafting table from five steel blocks, three glass
  and a chest; the hull and an engine there give the packed submarine. The second recipe's hull
  ingredient checks the chassis's vehicle component (`neoforge:components`), since every vehicle's
  chassis is the same item.
- **The sound**: an electric ferry's motor (CC0), a hum on 127 Hz, for a research submarine's
  electric thrusters (`devtools/art/sounds/SOURCES.md`).

**Why**:
- Alvin is the research submarine; its proportions read as one at a glance.
- A shell is what makes a cabin: the crew are inside, looking out of ports, as in the real thing.

**Alternatives rejected**:
- *A hull of hand-placed bands* (the Huey's way): a body rounded in both directions is many bands
  each way; the voxel shell is one predicate.
- *The hull in the cockpit* (hidden from riders): Vanilla Wheels never paints a cockpit part, so a
  dyed hull would come out undyed from outside.

**Verification**:
- GameTests (8): the profiles; the recipes, and another vehicle's hull refused; a crafted Explorer
  set down on open water floats at its draft; it holds its depth, then floats; its top speed;
  four ride and breathe at depth and a fifth is refused; the hold through the deck hatch and from a
  seat; a torpedo from each chin tube.
- Mutations of the shipped data, each caught: the recipe taking any hull; the stern's hit box
  removed (first missed: the test's 30-degree yaw let the body's box cover the hatch); the draft.
- The booth (17 checks): it floats at its draft, its paint shows, a real Left Shift dives it and
  Shift held keeps the pilot aboard, the screws turn, it holds its depth, the pilot breathes, the
  pilot and an observer see water through their ports, the floods light the seabed at night, the
  use key fires a torpedo, R lets the pilot out at the sail's hatch, the fit-out's four and two
  slots, the hold's three rows.
